ⓘ Time traveled = arrival local date and time minus departure local date and time. No timezone adjustment.

For each cross-timezone flight, the tracker subtracts the departure local calendar date and clock time from the arrival local date and clock time, without applying a timezone offset. A negative result is backward; a positive result is forward. The lifetime total adds the absolute results, and net is forward minus backward. Timezones and daylight saving rules are used only to verify actual flight duration and cross-timezone eligibility.

Example: HKG on December 25 at 06:00 to SFO on December 25 at 02:00 is 4 hours back on the local clocks. The actual flight took 12 hours, but the clock result stays −4 hours. When daylight saving makes a clock time repeat, choose the correct occurrence. This is a personal log, not a flight schedule verifier.
