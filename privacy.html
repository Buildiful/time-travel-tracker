Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Your flight log

Flight details and calculated totals are processed inside your browser. The tracker does not upload your flight history to Buildiful or any cloud database. Your current entries are automatically saved in your browser’s local storage. Save project downloads a JSON file to your device; Open project reads a JSON file locally. Clearing browser data can erase the automatic draft, so retain a downloaded project file if you want a backup.

The bundled airport list, time calculations, interface sounds and background music are loaded locally with the page. Timezone rules are supplied by your browser and may vary with browser updates. Background music is off by default and starts only when you turn it on.

Basic website analytics

On the official Buildiful GitHub Pages site, we use Google Analytics 4 to count page visits and a small set of button actions (adding/removing a flight, opening/saving a project, clearing the log, enabling music). We do not intentionally send flight records, airport codes, dates, times, project files, filenames or query parameters in analytics events. Our GA4 tag denies advertising consent and disables Google Signals and advertising personalization; associated Google Analytics property settings require separate configuration.

When enabled, GA4 may use first-party cookies, including pseudonymous identifiers, and may process technical browser and network information from which an approximate location can be derived. See Google’s Privacy Policy and GA4 cookies.

Regional privacy choices

In regions requiring prior consent for this analytics configuration, you can allow or decline before Google Analytics loads. In other recognized regions, basic analytics may begin without a prompt. We honor supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling GA4. If the regional check fails, analytics stays off unless you explicitly opt in.

Regional check and local preferences

To determine the regional analytics experience, the site may request https://ipwho.is/. That service can receive your request IP address. Only the coarse consent policy category is cached locally for up to 12 hours. Explicit choices are saved for up to 365 days and can be shared with other Buildiful tools on the same site. No full IP response or location coordinates are saved by this module.

Revoking permission stops subsequent measurement from this module; already collected data is not automatically deleted, and GA cookies already set may remain until cleared or expired. GA4 retention depends on the account’s configuration.

Hosting and contact

This site is delivered by GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process ordinary server requests independently of our analytics. For inquiries or applicable privacy rights, use the contact options on Buildiful’s website.
